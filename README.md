# Brewfolio

Your recipes. Your daily ritual.

Brewfolio is a mobile-first personal recipe web app for making a consistent cup of coffee. It brings saved recipes, dose-based pouring guides, and a brew timer into one simple brewing experience.

## The brewing experience

- Browse your saved recipes, starting with filter coffee.
- Adjust your coffee dose within the recipe's recommended range and see total water, individual pours, and cumulative scale targets recalculate.
- Follow recipe timings and a target finish window with a timer you can start, pause, and reset.
- Refer to the recipe's fixed ratio, water temperature, and grind guidance while brewing.
- Restore recipe defaults whenever you need a fresh start.
- See recipe sources and attribution alongside your saved recipes.

Grind guidance includes a description, approximate micron range, and Comandante C40 clicks with the axle type specified where supported by recipe sources. These are practical references, not exact conversions between grinders.

## Recipe collection

Recipes are maintained as structured files. The brewing interface focuses on following a recipe; creation and editing are outside its initial scope. The recipe model will support other drink types as the collection grows.

## Availability

Brewfolio is in development. The features above describe the intended application; a deployable version is not available yet.
